Incubated inside Lodestar CAIO.  Evidence before automation · Model-agnostic · Human authority first.

The hard part of property work is rarely generating another paragraph. It is determining what the property actually is, which source is authoritative, what conflicts, what evidence is missing, and what still requires professional judgment.

Lodestar Labs is turning that problem into a product discipline: observe first, preserve provenance, surface conflicts, and make uncertainty explicit before consequential decisions are made.

The problem

Property evidence is fragmented before the decision even starts.

  • Orders, MLS, public records, zoning, permits, title, documents, and field observations can disagree.
  • Complexity is often discovered after the assignment is already underway.
  • Professionals repeatedly reconstruct the same property from different systems.
  • Missing evidence can be mistaken for certainty when software forces an answer.
  • Reviewers often see the conclusion without a clean trail of how the underlying facts were established.

The product thesis

Build the evidence layer before automating the decision.

  • Observe approved sources without assuming any one source is automatically correct.
  • Reconcile competing observations into explicit claims and conflicts.
  • Flag missing evidence, unusual property characteristics, and assignment complexity.
  • Prepare inspection and review questions before they become revision problems.
  • Preserve provenance so the professional can see why a fact is trusted — or why it remains unresolved.

Product boundary: Lodestar Labs is not building an AVM or an automated appraisal-conclusion engine. The current product work focuses on evidence orchestration, complexity detection, inspection preparation, research reconciliation, and auditable decision support.

Validation loop

Built against real property workflows, not synthetic demos.

The current pilot follows a property from preflight through field observation, post-inspection research, comparable evidence, and final reconciliation.

A

Preflight

Establish property identity, source conflicts, missing documents, legal/use questions, and inspection priorities before substantive valuation analysis.

B

Field Delta

Compare what the evidence predicted with what the professional actually observes, separating confirmations, resolved conflicts, new findings, and true preflight misses.

C

Research & Market Delta

Add permits, zoning, listing history, market evidence, and comparable analysis while preserving the line between source-supported facts and professional judgment.

Operating principles

Trust is an architectural requirement.

  • Evidence before automation.
  • Conflicts are surfaced, not silently resolved.
  • UNRESOLVED is a valid result.
  • Deterministic rules before model judgment.
  • Models are replaceable reasoning engines, not the source of truth.
  • Human authority remains explicit at consequential boundaries.

Where this can go

Start with appraisal. Build for the broader property decision stack.

The beachhead is residential appraisal because it provides a high-information, regulated environment for proving the evidence architecture. The longer-term hypothesis is a reusable evidence layer for institutional property workflows.
